### Mistake #1
### She's Blaming Her Body For Something Her Supplements Are Actually Doing

Here's what nobody tells her when she starts stacking supplements to hit her macros.

That "zero sugar" pre-workout she mixes before every session is sweetened with sucralose, and her gut has no way to break sucralose down.

Those "low-carb" protein bars in her gym bag are sweetened with sugar alcohols like erythritol and xylitol. 

Her body can't absorb those either, so they pull water into her gut and sit there fermenting. 

That's the gas. The swelling she feels mid-set.

And the whey shake... The one her entire routine is built around, is loaded with lactose. 

Hardly anyone makes enough of the enzyme that breaks lactose down, so after two or three shakes it just ferments in her gut like everything else.

She isn't eating badly. 

She's eating exactly what the fitness industry told her to eat, and those products were designed to hit a macro number on a label, not to work with a human gut.
