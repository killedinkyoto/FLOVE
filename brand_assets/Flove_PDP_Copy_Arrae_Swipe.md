**I've tried three probiotics. Why is this one different?**
> Because the other three likely never made it out of your stomach. Standard gelatin and HPMC capsules dissolve at pH 1.5 to 3.5, which means the cultures spill into gastric acid and die within minutes. You paid premium prices for freeze-dried protein. **Will this cramp me mid-lift like my greens powder did?**
